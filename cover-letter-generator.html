Generate a Cover Letter — Free How it works

A cover letter that opens with "To Whom It May Concern" and never mentions the role, the company, or anything specific about you rarely gets read past the first line. CareerCanvas's generator asks for the handful of details that actually matter — job title, company, hiring manager's name if you have it, your skills and experience, and the tone you want — and drafts a letter structured around them, so the starting point is already specific rather than generic filler you have to rewrite from scratch.

How the Cover Letter Generator works

1

2

Add your skills & experience

A short summary of your relevant background and strengths. The more specific this is, the more specific your draft will be.

Frequently asked questions

Will this guarantee me an interview?

No tool can guarantee that. What it does is remove the blank-page problem — you get a structured, specific starting draft in under a minute that you should still personalize before sending.

Will the letter sound generic?

The draft is built from the exact job title, company and skills you enter, so it is more specific than a static template — but we'd still recommend adding one personal detail about why you want that particular role.

Can I edit the generated text?

Yes, the whole draft is a plain editable text area — change any sentence, add a paragraph, or shorten it before downloading.

Is this free?

Yes, generating and downloading cover letters is completely free with a CareerCanvas account.

Do I need a hiring manager's name?

No — leave it blank and the generator uses a neutral, still-professional greeting instead of a generic "To Whom It May Concern."
