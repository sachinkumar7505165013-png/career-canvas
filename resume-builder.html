How the Resume Builder works

Four steps, start to finish — most people have a complete, downloadable resume in under fifteen minutes.

1

Pick a template

Browse 15 designs across Professional, Modern, Photo and ATS-Friendly categories. Every template is a real layout difference — timelines, boxed sections, split headers, dark sidebars — not just a recolored version of the same page.

2

Fill in your details

Work through clear tabs for contact info, objective, education, experience, skills, projects, certifications, achievements and more. Demo content is pre-filled so every field shows you a realistic example as you go.

3

Fine-tune the look

Adjust font, font size, line spacing, margins and accent color from the live preview. The builder automatically shrinks spacing to keep everything on one page, so you don't have to manually fight an overflowing section.

4

Download your PDF

Export a print-ready PDF with even 10mm margins on every side. Page breaks are nudged to land between lines, not through the middle of one, even on longer resumes.

Frequently asked questions

Is the resume builder really free?

Yes. Every template, every download and every feature in the builder is free. CareerCanvas has no paid plans — you only need a free account (email or Google) to save your draft.

Do I need Microsoft Word to use my resume afterward?

No. You build and preview your resume entirely in the browser, then download it as a ready-to-send PDF — nothing to install.

Can I switch templates after I've filled in my details?

Yes. Your content is stored separately from the template design, so you can preview the same resume across different templates and pick whichever looks best before downloading.

Is my resume data safe?

Your draft is saved to your free account and only you can access it — it's never sold or shared with third parties. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can freshers with no work experience use this?

Yes — several templates and the "education-first" section ordering are built for students and freshers who are leading with academics and projects rather than job history.
